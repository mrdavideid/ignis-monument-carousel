## Como West Public School Staff Amenities

An NSW Department of Education new construction project. Standalone staff amenities block, brick veneer, pitched corrugated roof, accessible WC, kitchenette, end-of-trip facilities.

A staff amenities block looks simple until you read the brief. Compliant accessible WC. Hydraulic services tied to existing site infrastructure. Roof drainage planned against the existing stormwater system. Building Code of Australia compliance documented at every stage.

Construction was sequenced against the school year. External shell raised during break periods. Internal fit-out continued during term with separation from staff and student traffic. ISO 9001 quality controls in BuildPass from day one.

Amenities handed over commissioned and signed off. Staff using it the week of return. No retrofit visits, no reactive defects.

Constructive thinking.
