## Edgeware School Whole-of-School Landscape

An NSW Department of Education whole-of-school landscape upgrade at Edgeware School, a specialist behavioural school for high-needs students.

45 students full-time, all with behavioural support needs, all in attendance, no shutdown window. Every surface of the school in scope. The principal's opening question was direct: how were we going to work inside the school across the term without setting the students off.

We answered with the live and high-risk environment doctrine in full. Programme built around student movement and the school day. Continuous separation between work zone and student zone, monitored hour to hour. Director on the phone with the principal. Subcontractors briefed daily before tools came out.

Whole-of-school landscape delivered on programme. Zero complaints, zero incidents, full school activity uninterrupted across the build. The proof point that makes the live-environment claim non-hand-waved.

Constructive thinking.
