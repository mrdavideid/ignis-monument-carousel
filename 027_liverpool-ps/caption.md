## Liverpool Public School Playground

An NSW Department of Education playgrounds and landscape upgrade at Liverpool Public School. New play infrastructure, soft-fall surfacing, landscape works, and accessible perimeter paths.

Playgrounds in primary schools are not amenity. They are the working environment of every child between bell to bell. The build has to be safe, accessible, and durable enough to absorb the use it will get.

We delivered the playground in a school-aware programme. Bulk works in the holiday window. Soft-fall and equipment installation timed against the cure schedules in the spec. ISO 45001 safety controls visible at every interface with school staff.

Playground in service from the first morning of the term. Kids on the new surface within hours of the bell. Defect Liability Period actively managed across the year that follows.

Constructive thinking.
