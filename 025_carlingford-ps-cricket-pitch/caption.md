## Carlingford Public School Cricket Pitch

An NSW Department of Education sports facilities upgrade. New cricket pitch at Carlingford Public School, designed for both PE programme use and after-school cricket club use.

A primary school cricket pitch needs to be safe, durable, and dimensionally correct. The wicket base, the playing surface, the surrounds all have to comply with school sports requirements without over-engineering the project.

We delivered the pitch to a school-aware programme. Base preparation in the holiday window. Synthetic surface set with the right surface tolerances. Final line marking signed off with the school's PE coordinator before handover.

Pitch in service for the next term. Used in PE, used by the cricket club, used in the community sessions after school. The structure under it will outlast the players on it.

Constructive thinking.
