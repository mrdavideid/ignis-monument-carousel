## Busby West Public School Amenities

An NSW Department of Education new construction project. New amenities block at Busby West Public School, designed and delivered to current Department standards.

Busby West is an active primary school with a tight footprint. The new amenities had to fit a constrained site, tie into existing services, and be operational without disrupting the daily school routine.

Programme was sequenced around school operations. Bulk works in school holidays. Internal fit-out windows scheduled with the principal in the loop. ISO 14001 environmental controls in place, especially around the playground edge where dust and noise required active management.

Amenities commissioned, accessibility verified, handed across to the school. Operational from week one of the next term, no follow-up visits required.

Constructive thinking.
