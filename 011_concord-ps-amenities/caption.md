## Concord Public School Amenities

An NSW Department of Education new construction project. New amenities block at Concord Public School, delivered to current Department standards for school amenities.

A school amenities build is a procurement-compliant project before it is a construction project. Compliance with the Department of Education's design standards. Accessibility under AS 1428. Hydraulic systems specified to scheme requirements. Documentation pack that has to survive future audits.

We delivered the block on a school-aware programme. Slab and shell raised during the holiday period. Internal trades sequenced with director-led oversight, BuildPass-tracked, and the full ISO 9001 quality file generated automatically as work progressed.

Block in service the morning of return. Compliant, durable, defect-managed. A specification that the school can hand on to the next principal without explanation.

Constructive thinking.
