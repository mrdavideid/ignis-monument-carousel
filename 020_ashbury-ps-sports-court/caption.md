## Ashbury Public School Sports Court

An NSW Department of Education sports court upgrade at Ashbury Public School. Existing surface refurbished, line markings refreshed, perimeter elements re-set against the design intent.

Refurbishment is its own discipline. You inherit the surface, the structure, the drainage path, the kerb returns. The work has to leave the asset better than new without breaking the existing tie-ins that were never going to be replaced.

We staged the refurb across the holiday and weekend windows. Existing surface assessed and treated, not torn out. New line markings set with the school's PE staff sign-off. ISO 14001 environmental controls in place across every stage of the works.

Court back in service. The structure under it is more durable than the day we arrived. School day uninterrupted across the programme.

Constructive thinking.
