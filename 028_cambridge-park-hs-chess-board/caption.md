## Cambridge Park HS Outdoor Chess Board

An NSW Department of Education playgrounds and landscape feature project. Outdoor giant chess board and supporting landscape at Cambridge Park High School.

A built feature like an outdoor chess board has to read as a place. The squares have to be set to playing dimensions. The surrounding paths have to flow. The seating has to invite kids to use it before, during, and after PE.

We delivered the chess board and its landscape to a school-aware programme. Concrete pour set with grid accuracy. Painted squares applied to playing tolerances. Surrounding paths flowing into existing student movement patterns. ISO 14001 environmental management active across the works.

Chess board in service from the first break of the term. Used every day, in every direction.

Constructive thinking.
