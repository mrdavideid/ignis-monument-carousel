## Bransgrove Rd Depot Signwriters Building

A specialist trade facility delivered at an operational government works depot. Heavy-duty industrial shell, exposed portal frame, integrated dust extraction, large roller access for vehicle-side workflow.

The depot keeps running. Crews come and go through the gates every morning. Materials, plant, vehicles, all routed past the construction zone. The signwriters need to be operating in the new building from day one of handover.

We delivered the structure in coordination with the depot's working programme. Subcontractors inducted through BuildPass with depot-specific protocols. ISO 45001 safety management visible across every interface with operational depot staff.

Building handed over with dust extraction commissioned, services live, and workflow tested. The trade started on day one without a transition period.

Constructive thinking.
