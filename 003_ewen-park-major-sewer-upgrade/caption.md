## Ewen Park Major Sewer Upgrade

A civil works upgrade at Ewen Park. Trench excavation, rock removal, pipe lay, road and surface reinstatement, all delivered inside a live public park environment.

Open trenches sit beside a working park. Residents walk past, dogs are off lead, the field above is in use. Every metre of trench gets perimeter mesh, signage, and a continuous reinstatement plan that closes the work behind us as we move.

This is the live-environment doctrine applied to civil. Programme calibrated around park access. ISO 45001 safety controls maintained continuously, not just at induction. Director on site at every milestone.

Pipe in. Surface reinstated. Park back to the community without a chapter of disruption attached to it.

Constructive thinking.
