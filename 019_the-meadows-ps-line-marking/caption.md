## The Meadows Public School Line Marking

An NSW Department of Education sports facilities upgrade. Comprehensive line marking refresh across primary school sports court, including standard playing courts, fun games, and pre-school markings.

Line marking looks like the smallest job on a school site. Until you visit a school where it is wrong. Faded markings affect every PE class, every break, every after-school session. They get used, every day, by a lot of kids.

We delivered the line marking to the school's specification in a single weekend window. Existing surface cleaned and prepared. Lines applied with the spec sheet checked against every shape. Quality file generated through BuildPass for the asset record.

Markings in service the Monday after the works. Cricket pitch, basketball half-court, four square, hopscotch, all back to the kids and ready for the term.

Constructive thinking.
