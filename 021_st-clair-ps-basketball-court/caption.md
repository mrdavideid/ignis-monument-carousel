## St Clair Public School Basketball Court

An NSW Department of Education sports facilities upgrade. New basketball court at St Clair Public School, including asphalt base, line marking, and cantilevered hoop installation.

A school basketball court is a community asset. It gets used during recess, during PE, after the bell, on weekends. The build has to hold up under all of that load, year after year.

We delivered the court to specification across a holiday and weekend programme. Asphalt base laid to playing tolerances. Cantilevered hoops bolted to engineered concrete pads. Line markings applied to the standard primary school dimensions. ISO 9001 quality file generated at every milestone.

Court signed off and ready. Kids on the new surface from day one of the term. The structure under the court will outlast the kids on it.

Constructive thinking.
