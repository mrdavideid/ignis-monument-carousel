## Ebenezar Public School Asphalt Upgrade

An NSW Department of Education civil works programme at Ebenezar Public School. Existing asphalt removed, subgrade prepared, new surface laid with painted line markings and integrated drainage.

A school asphalt rebuild is a programme problem before it is a paving problem. The school cannot lose its main movement zone for a week. The surface has to be ready for kids by Monday morning.

We sequenced the work around weekends and holiday windows. Subgrade and base in the first window. Hot mix and line marking in the second. Continuous site separation between work zones and the rest of the school across the full programme.

Surface in. Drainage holding under the first storm. Programme handed back to the principal, not extended around them.

Constructive thinking.
