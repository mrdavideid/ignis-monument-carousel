## Memorial Oval Change Rooms Upgrade

A Canterbury Bankstown City Council fit out and refurbishment project. Change rooms upgrade at Memorial Oval, supporting cricket and the broader community use of the precinct.

Memorial Oval change rooms are a high-rotation council asset. Multiple clubs, multiple codes, multiple weekends a season. The refurb has to lift the standard without taking the venue offline.

We delivered the upgrade to Council's specification. Programme staged around the sports calendar. Tile, fixtures, partitions, and accessibility installed against AS 1428. ISO 14001 environmental management active across waste, water, and dust. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass.

Change rooms back in service for the season. Council's asset is more durable than the day we arrived. A long-standing relationship reinforced through delivery.

Constructive thinking.
