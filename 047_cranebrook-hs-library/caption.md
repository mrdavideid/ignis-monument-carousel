## Cranebrook High School Library

An NSW Department of Education library refurbishment at Cranebrook High School. Internal works including joinery, finishes, services, accessibility upgrades, and integrated technology.

A high school library is more than a book room. It is a study space, a digital workspace, a quiet zone, and a teaching environment. The fit-out has to support all of those uses, in the same footprint, every period of the day.

We delivered the library through a holiday-window programme. Joinery installed to spec. Services and data live before students returned. Accessibility verified against AS 1428. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass.

Library in service from day one of return. Students using the new space across study, group work, and quiet reading. A teaching asset back to full capacity.
