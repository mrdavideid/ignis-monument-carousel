## GRC Peakhurst Amenities & Changeroom Upgrade

A Georges River Council amenities and changeroom refurbishment at the Peakhurst precinct. Tile, fixtures, partitions, accessibility, and supporting works delivered inside a live recreational facility.

Changeroom refurbishments are wet works, finishing works, accessibility works, and access works all in one programme. The site is used by clubs and the public around the build. Council is the owner and the auditor.

We delivered the refurbishment to Council's specification. Programme staged to respect the venue's bookings. Tile and fixture works completed inside the agreed shutdown windows. Accessibility verified against AS 1428. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass at every milestone.

Changerooms back in service for clubs and the public. Council's asset is more durable than the day we arrived.
