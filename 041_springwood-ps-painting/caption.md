## Springwood Public School Painting Programme

An NSW Department of Education fit-out and refurbishment programme. Painting works across Springwood Public School, including classrooms, corridors, external works, and supporting trades.

A school painting programme is a sequencing problem before it is a finishing problem. Every classroom has to be available the week the teacher needs it. Every corridor has to be safe the morning the kids walk down it. Every external face has to be weatherproofed by the next storm.

We delivered the works to a school-aware programme. Internal works through holiday and weekend windows. ISO 45001 safety controls maintained continuously across every interface with school staff and students. Quality file built through BuildPass.

Programme handed back to the school. Classrooms in service for the term. External works holding to the spec they were signed off against.

Constructive thinking.
