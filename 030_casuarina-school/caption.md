## Casuarina School Landscape

An NSW Department of Education playgrounds and landscape upgrade at Casuarina School. Landscape works integrating with the school's existing learning environments.

Specialist school landscapes are not a generic spec problem. The students have specific needs. The staff have specific operational patterns. The result has to support both, every day, for the life of the asset.

We delivered the landscape in close coordination with the school. Programme set around the school calendar. ISO 45001 safety controls visible across every interface with students and staff. Director engaged with the principal at every milestone.

Landscape in service. Used the way the school needs it to be used. Defect Liability Period actively managed for the year that follows.

Constructive thinking.
