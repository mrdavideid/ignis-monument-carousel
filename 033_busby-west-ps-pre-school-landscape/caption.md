## Busby West PS Pre-School Landscape

An NSW Department of Education landscape works project at Busby West Public School's pre-school facility. Soft landscape, hard paths, garden zones, and inclusive play surfaces.

A pre-school landscape lives at a different scale. The thresholds are lower. The materials are softer. The supervision sightlines are tighter. The brief has to be respected from the kerb to the back fence.

We delivered the landscape to the pre-school's operational requirements. Programme staged through the holiday window. Soft-fall surfaces installed to AS 4422 standards. Plant choices verified against the early-childhood safety register. ISO 14001 environmental management active across the works.

Landscape in service for the next intake. Used as designed, supervised as designed, durable for the years it will be carrying.

Constructive thinking.
