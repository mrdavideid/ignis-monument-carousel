## Coogee Public School Amphitheatre and Sports Court

An NSW Department of Education combined works project at Coogee Public School. Outdoor amphitheatre construction integrated with the existing sports court, supporting both performance and sport use of the same outdoor footprint.

Combined-use spaces are harder than single-use. The seating geometry has to read for assembly. The court has to read for sport. The transition between the two has to be safe, durable, and clear.

We delivered the works to a single integrated programme. Amphitheatre seating built to AS 3600 concrete standards. Court surface and line markings applied to school sports spec. Tactile threshold transitions through every level change. ISO 14001 environmental management active across the works.

Amphitheatre and court signed off and in use. The school runs assembly on Monday and basketball on Tuesday. The footprint earns its space.

Constructive thinking.
