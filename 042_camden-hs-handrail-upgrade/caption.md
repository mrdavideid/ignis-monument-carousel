## Camden High School Handrail Upgrade

An NSW Department of Education access works and refurbishment project. Compliant handrail upgrade across stairs, ramps, and transition zones at Camden High School.

Handrail compliance reads small on a tender brief. It reads large on a site safety inspection. AS 1428 dimensions, AS 1657 structural loading, fixings against the substrate available, all have to be delivered without a chapter of redesign at the site.

We delivered the upgrade across a term-aware programme. Existing structure surveyed. New rail set to AS 1428 dimensions. Fixings engineered against each substrate type. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass at every stair flight and ramp run.

Handrails in service the morning after each section was signed off. Compliant, durable, and ready for the load every student in the school puts on them every day.

Constructive thinking.
