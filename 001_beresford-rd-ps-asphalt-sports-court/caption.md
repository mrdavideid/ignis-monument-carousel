## Beresford Rd Public School Sports Court

An NSW Department of Education sports court at an operational primary school. New black asphalt playing surface, painted line markings, galvanised cantilevered sports posts, sandstone perimeter seating.

The school stays open. The court has to be delivered around term time, the morning bell, recess movement, after-school programmes. Every barrier, every delivery, every subcontractor sequenced against the school day before the work begins.

Live and high-risk delivery is the default setting. The programme is built around the bell, not the other way around. Director on the phone with the principal. Subcontractors briefed daily before tools come out.

Court back in service for the kids. Zero complaints, zero incidents, full school activity uninterrupted across the build.

Constructive thinking.
