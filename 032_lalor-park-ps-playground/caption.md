## Lalor Park Public School Playground

An NSW Department of Education playgrounds and landscape upgrade at Lalor Park Public School. New play equipment, soft-fall surfacing, accessible paths, and integrated landscape works.

A primary school playground is the most heavily used space on the site. Every kid uses it every day. The equipment, the surface, the layout, the accessibility, all judged by the load they actually carry.

We delivered the playground in the holiday window. Equipment installation to manufacturer and AS 4685 specifications. Soft-fall depth verified at every fall zone. Accessible paths set with AS 1428 compliance for every threshold. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass at every step.

Playground in service from day one of return. Used by every child, every break, every day. A facility that holds up under the use it gets.

Constructive thinking.
