## Cabramatta Public School Canteen

An NSW Department of Education canteen fit-out and refurbishment at Cabramatta Public School. Service infrastructure, joinery, finishes, and food-grade surfaces delivered to current Department and Food Authority standards.

A school canteen build is a compliance project. NSW Food Authority signoff sits at the top. The joinery, the splashbacks, the hand-wash stations, the floor drainage, all have to be specified and verified for the use the canteen will see every day.

We delivered the canteen to the Department's standards and the regulator's expectations. Hydraulic and electrical services routed to existing infrastructure. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass. Director engaged with the principal at every milestone.

Canteen open on day one of the term. P&C operating from morning bell. Food service compliant, durable, and ready for the year.

Constructive thinking.
