## Parry Park Clubhouse, Stage 2

A Canterbury Bankstown City Council fit out and refurbishment project. Stage 2 of the Parry Park clubhouse works, completing the internal fit-out and finishes following Stage 1's slab and shell.

Stage 2 is where the building becomes a clubhouse. Joinery, finishes, services commissioned, accessibility verified. Stage 1's tie-ins have to work as planned. The result has to be ready for the community on day one of handover.

We delivered Stage 2 to Council's specification. Joinery installed to spec. Services commissioned and tested. Accessibility verified against AS 1428. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass.

Clubhouse handed across complete. Community using the facility the weekend after handover. Stage 1 and Stage 2 read as one project, because they were managed as one project.

Constructive thinking.
