## Rosehill Public School Artificial Turf

An NSW Department of Education landscape works upgrade. Artificial turf installation at Rosehill Public School, replacing worn natural turf in a high-traffic central play zone.

Artificial turf at this scale is a base works project before it is a surface project. Subgrade preparation. Drainage tie-ins. Shock pad layer. Then the turf, joined and seamed to playing tolerance.

We delivered the turf in a programme staged through the holiday window. Base prepared to specification. Turf installed and tested before students returned. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass with every milestone recorded.

Turf in service. Kids using it every break. A landscape that will hold up under the load a primary school throws at it, year after year.

Constructive thinking.
