A council programme. Three community centre refurbishments delivered as a single co-ordinated body of work, including internal and external works across all three sites.

Programme-level work is where the boutique-by-design Monument model earns its place. One director on the pulse across three sites. One quality file, one safety file, one defect schedule across the programme. Council gets the consistency it needs without losing the responsiveness it expects.
