## Beresford Rd Public School Sports Court (Sports Facilities)

An NSW Department of Education sports court at Beresford Rd Public School. New asphalt playing surface, painted line markings in standard school colours, galvanised cantilevered sports posts.

Sports facilities at schools live a double life. They are sport during school hours and community recreation outside of them. The build has to respect both audiences.

We delivered the court to a school-aware programme. Subgrade preparation and base course in the holiday window. Line marking and equipment installation timed against the term calendar. ISO 9001 quality controls visible in BuildPass at every stage, from subgrade approval to final line measurement.

Court back in service. Sport happening on the new surface within hours of handover. A facility that holds up under daily wear and the after-school community use that follows.

Constructive thinking.
