## Busby West Public School Canteen

An NSW Department of Education fit out and refurbishment project. Canteen upgrade at Busby West Public School, including service infrastructure, joinery, finishes, and compliant food preparation surfaces.

A school canteen is a regulated food premises before it is a fit-out. NSW Food Authority compliance. Hand-wash stations specified to code. Joinery surfaces that have to be cleanable to a standard the regulator can check.

We delivered the canteen to the Department's standards and the regulator's expectations. Hydraulic and electrical services tied to existing infrastructure. Joinery installed to a level the P&C can rely on for years. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass at every milestone.

Canteen open for the first day of term. P&C operating with confidence. Food service compliant, durable, and ready for the school year.
