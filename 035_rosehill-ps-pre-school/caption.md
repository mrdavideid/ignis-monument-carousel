## Rosehill PS Pre-School Landscape

An NSW Department of Education landscape works project at Rosehill Public School's pre-school. Soft surfaces, play zones, paths, and supporting plant.

Pre-school landscapes get judged by the staff who supervise them, not the parents who walk past them. Sightlines, safety, durability, accessibility, all have to work in the moment a child runs in any direction.

We delivered the landscape to the school's specifications. Programme set across the holiday window. Soft-fall surfaces installed to AS 4422. Plant choices and finishes verified against the early-childhood safety register. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass at every milestone.

Landscape in service for the next intake. Used the way the staff need it to be used.

Constructive thinking.
