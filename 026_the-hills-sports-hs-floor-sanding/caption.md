## The Hills Sports HS Floor Sanding

An NSW Department of Education sports facility refurbishment. Comprehensive floor sanding and recoating of the indoor sports hall at The Hills Sports High School.

Indoor sports surfaces have a finite life before they need to be reset. The grain has to be opened. The level has to be checked. The new coat has to cure to a playable finish under the school's sport calendar.

We delivered the works inside the school's holiday window. Existing floor assessed and prepared. Multiple cure cycles managed against the season schedule. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass at every stage, signed off by the school's sports coordinator.

Floor in service for the next term. Trials and sport back on the new surface from day one of return.

Constructive thinking.
