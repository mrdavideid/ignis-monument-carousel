## Currans Hill Public School Soccer Field

An NSW Department of Education sports facilities upgrade. Soccer field reconstruction at Currans Hill Public School, including levelling, sub-surface drainage, synthetic or turf surface, and perimeter works.

A school soccer field reconstruction is two projects in one. The earthworks below the surface, where the levels and drainage live. The playing surface above, where the result is judged.

We delivered the field in a programme staged through holiday and break windows. Subgrade levelled and drained to AS 3500 specifications. Playing surface installed and given the cure window it needed before students returned. ISO 14001 environmental controls active across the bulk works.

Field signed off and in service. Sport happening on the new surface within the first PE block of the term.

Constructive thinking.
