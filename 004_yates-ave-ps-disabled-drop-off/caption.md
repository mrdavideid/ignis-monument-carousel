## Yates Ave Public School Disabled Drop-Off

An NSW Department of Education access works upgrade. Compliant disabled drop-off bay, kerb modifications, tactile ground surface indicators, gradient transitions designed against AS 1428.

Access works are the kind of project that has to be invisible after handover. The kerb has to drop at the right rate. The pram ramp has to land where wheelchairs and prams need it to land. The line marking has to read for the parent in the rain at 8:32 in the morning.

We delivered the works to the school's drop-off cycle. Mobilisation outside core hours. Concrete cures planned around school holidays. The new bay was in service the morning after pour completion.

Compliant, durable, used every day. The reason it works is because the detail was respected at the kerb.

Constructive thinking.
