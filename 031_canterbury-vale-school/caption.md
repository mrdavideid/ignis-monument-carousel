## Canterbury Vale School Landscape

An NSW Department of Education whole-of-school landscape upgrade at Canterbury Vale School, a specialist behavioural support school.

Specialist behavioural schools demand more, not less, from a builder. The students have specific sensitivities. The staff have specific safety constraints. The site has to feel calm, ordered, and durable from the first minute of the first day back.

We delivered the landscape under a live-environment doctrine. Programme built around the school day. Continuous separation between work zone and student zone. Subcontractors briefed daily on the operational constraints before tools came out. Director on the phone with the principal at every milestone.

Landscape handed across. Zero incidents involving students. Zero operational complaints. The school day uninterrupted across the programme.

Constructive thinking.
