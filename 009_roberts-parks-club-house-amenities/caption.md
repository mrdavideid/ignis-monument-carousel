## Roberts Park Clubhouse and Amenities

A Canterbury Bankstown City Council new construction project. Clubhouse and amenities at Roberts Park, sports infrastructure supporting community use.

Roberts Park is one of the most heavily used recreational assets in Canterbury Bankstown. The brief required new clubhouse facilities and supporting amenities, delivered without taking the park offline.

We delivered the works to Council's programme. Tactile ground surface indicators set at every threshold for accessibility. Compliant disabled access. Galvanised handrails, brick veneer to match the existing precinct character, ISO 14001 environmental controls maintained across the build.

Clubhouse handed across to Council. Community already using the facility on the weekend after handover. A long-standing relationship reinforced through delivery, not promised in a tender.

Constructive thinking.
