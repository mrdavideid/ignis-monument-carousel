## GRC Penshurst Amenities Upgrade

A Georges River Council amenities refurbishment at the Penshurst precinct. Refurbishment of public amenities with full accessibility compliance and durable wet area finishes.

Public amenities are a high-rotation asset. Used by the community day after day. The finishes have to be durable, easy to clean, and compliant for the life of the asset, not just the warranty period.

We delivered the upgrade to Council's specification. Programme staged around precinct use. Tile, fixtures, and accessibility set against AS 1428. ISO 14001 environmental management active across waste, water, and dust. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass.

Amenities back in service for the community. Council's procurement file is defensible, the asset is documented, the Defect Liability Period is actively managed.

Constructive thinking.
