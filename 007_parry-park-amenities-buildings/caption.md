## Parry Park Amenities Buildings

A Canterbury Bankstown City Council new construction project. New amenities buildings at Parry Park, supporting the existing sports and community use of the precinct.

Council amenities are public infrastructure on public timelines. The community uses the park while construction is in motion. Soccer fixtures, junior football, weekend events, all running around the site fence.

We treated Parry Park as a live environment from the planning stage. Programme built around the sports calendar. Hoarding placed to keep sightlines into the park while keeping the work zone secure. Director engaged with Council through every milestone.

Amenities delivered to the spec the community expected. Building handed across to Council with a complete documentation pack and an actively managed Defect Liability Period.

Constructive thinking.
