## Connells Point PS Chair Lift

An NSW Department of Education access works project at Connells Point Public School. Compliant chair lift installation enabling access between school levels for students and staff with mobility needs.

A chair lift in a school is access infrastructure that has to work every day. It cannot fail. It cannot be slow. It cannot be unavailable when the student who needs it arrives at the platform.

We delivered the installation to AS 1735 standards. Structural support engineered against the existing building. Electrical and controls commissioned and tested under load. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass. Training delivered to the school's operational staff before handover.

Lift in service from the day students returned. Compliant, durable, and operating the way the school needed it to.
