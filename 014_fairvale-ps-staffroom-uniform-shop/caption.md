## Fairvale Public School Staffroom & Uniform Shop

An NSW Department of Education new construction project. Standalone staffroom and uniform shop facility at Fairvale Public School.

The brief had two functions on one footprint. A staffroom that staff would actually use. A uniform shop that the P&C could run efficiently with parents coming in and out. Both had to be compliant, accessible, and durable.

We delivered the building to the Department's standards across both functions. Hydraulic, mechanical, and electrical services routed to suit both internal layouts. Programme sequenced through school holidays for external works and active management of any in-term internal works.

Building commissioned and handed over with both functions ready to run. Staff in the new staffroom from day one of return. Uniform shop operating on the first scheduled day.

Constructive thinking.
