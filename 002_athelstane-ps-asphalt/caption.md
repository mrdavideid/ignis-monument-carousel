## Athelstane Public School Asphalt Works

An NSW Department of Education civil upgrade at Athelstane Public School. New asphalt playing surface, perimeter concrete kerb, line markings, integrated drainage.

The reality of school asphalt is not the surface. It is everything underneath. Subgrade preparation, drainage tie-ins, level transitions to the existing playground, kerb returns that have to hold under daily wear.

We work the programme around school operations. Bulk earthworks scheduled outside term. Hot mix laid in windows that respect the surrounding teaching spaces. ISO 14001 environmental controls in place from day one.

Surface handed back inside the agreed programme. School day uninterrupted. Defect Liability Period managed actively, not abandoned at handover.

Constructive thinking.
