## Glossodia Public School Canteen

An NSW Department of Education canteen fit-out and refurbishment at Glossodia Public School. Compliant food preparation infrastructure, joinery, finishes, and service connections.

A primary school canteen runs on volunteer hours and tight margins. The refurb has to make the P&C's job easier, not harder. Layout, services, surfaces, hand-wash positioning, all have to work the way the volunteers actually use the space.

We delivered the canteen to the Department's standards and the regulator's expectations. Services tied into existing site infrastructure. Joinery installed to a durable spec. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass.

Canteen open on day one of the term. P&C operating with confidence. The asset is documented for the next set of volunteers.

Constructive thinking.
