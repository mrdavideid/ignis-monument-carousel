## Belmore Sports Ground Bifold Doors

A Canterbury Bankstown City Council sports facility upgrade. New bifold door installation at Belmore Sports Ground, opening the training gym to the field and enabling indoor-outdoor use of the precinct.

Bifold doors at this scale are a structural intervention. The opening has to be reinforced, sealed, and flashing-detailed against weather. The hardware has to survive thousands of cycles a season under heavy daily use.

We delivered the works inside Council's sports calendar. Existing structure assessed. New lintel and supporting structure designed against AS 1170 loading. Bifolds installed and tested against the cycle expectations of the venue. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass.

Doors operational and tested. The training gym opens up to the field for every session. A facility that supports the way the venue actually gets used.

Constructive thinking.
