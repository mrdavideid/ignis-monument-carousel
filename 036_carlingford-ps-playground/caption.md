## Carlingford Public School Playground

An NSW Department of Education playground upgrade. New equipment, soft-fall, perimeter paths, and integrated landscape at Carlingford Public School.

Primary school playgrounds operate at full load from the first morning back. The equipment has to be in. The soft-fall has to be cured. The paths have to flow. The defect schedule has to be empty.

We delivered the playground inside the holiday window. AS 4685 equipment installations. AS 4422 fall surfacing. AS 1428 access paths. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass at every step, signed off by the school's principal before handover.

Playground in service from day one. Used every break. A facility that holds up under the load it carries.

Constructive thinking.
