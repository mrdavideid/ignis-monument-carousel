## Eagle Vale High School Cricket Pitch

An NSW Department of Education sports facilities upgrade. New cricket pitch and supporting infrastructure at Eagle Vale High School, delivered to school sports specifications.

Cricket pitches are deceptively technical. The wicket base, the synthetic or turf surface, the surrounds, the perimeter line marking, all have to comply with playing standards and survive the daily wear of a working school sports programme.

We delivered the pitch to specification on a school-aware programme. Base works staged in the holiday window. Synthetic surface installation timed to allow the cure window before students returned. Final line marking signed off against the school's sports staff before handover.

Pitch in service for the next season. Used every break, every PE class, and every after-school session. A facility built for the load it actually carries.

Constructive thinking.
