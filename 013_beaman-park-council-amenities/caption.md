## Beaman Park Council Amenities

A council new construction project. Amenities block at Beaman Park, supporting community sports and recreational use of the precinct.

Council amenities sit between the user (the public) and the asset owner (Council). Both audiences have to be respected. The community needs the facility operating to a high standard. Council needs the asset documented, compliant, and warrantable.

We delivered Beaman Park to a council-aligned programme. Brick veneer to match existing council precinct character. Compliant accessibility design through tactile thresholds and AS 1428 ramping. ISO 45001 safety controls maintained through every interface with park users.

Asset handed across with a complete certification pack and an actively managed Defect Liability Period. Council's procurement file is defensible, the community's facility is operating.

Constructive thinking.
