## Coleman Park Tennis Club Upgrade

A council amenities and clubhouse refurbishment at Coleman Park. Internal and external works supporting an active community tennis club.

Community sports clubs are not procurement abstractions. There are members who train every Tuesday, kids who run social comps on Saturday morning, volunteers who run the canteen. The refurb has to be invisible to that rhythm.

We delivered the works to Council's specification and the club's calendar. Programme staged around match days. ISO 45001 safety controls maintained continuously across every interface with members and the public. Director engaged with both Council and the club president at every milestone.

Clubhouse back in service. Members noticing the upgrade, not the disruption. A relationship reinforced through delivery.

Constructive thinking.
