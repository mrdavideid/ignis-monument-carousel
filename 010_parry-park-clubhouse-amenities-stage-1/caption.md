## Parry Park Clubhouse Amenities, Stage 1

A Canterbury Bankstown City Council new construction project. Stage 1 of the Parry Park clubhouse amenities works, sequenced to keep the field and adjacent facilities operational through the build.

Stage 1 is the foundation for everything that follows. The slab has to be accurate. The services have to come up where Stage 2 expects them. The hydraulic, electrical, and stormwater tie-ins have to be future-proofed for the second pass.

We co-ordinated the design intent against Council's long-term plan for Parry Park. Services planned against Stage 2 from the first set of drawings. Director engaged with Council through every design decision that affected the next stage.

Stage 1 handed across, ready to receive Stage 2 without rework. Council walked into the next pass with a working programme, not a salvage operation.

Constructive thinking.
