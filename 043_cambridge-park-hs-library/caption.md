## Cambridge Park High School Library

An NSW Department of Education library fit-out and refurbishment project at Cambridge Park High School. Internal works including joinery, finishes, services, accessibility, and integrated technology.

A school library refurb is teaching infrastructure. Joinery has to hold collection load. Cabling has to support student devices and the IT staff who will maintain them. Lighting and acoustics have to support quiet work. Accessibility has to be verified.

We delivered the library across a term-aware programme. Bulk works through the holiday window. Joinery installed to spec. Services live and commissioned. ISO 9001 quality file generated through BuildPass at every milestone.

Library in service from day one of return. Students using the space the way it was designed to be used. A teaching asset back at full capacity.

Constructive thinking.
