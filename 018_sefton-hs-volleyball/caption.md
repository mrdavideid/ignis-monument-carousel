## Sefton High School Volleyball Court

An NSW Department of Education sports facility upgrade. New indoor volleyball court installation at Sefton High School, including line marking, ancillary works, and surface preparation.

Indoor sports surfaces have to land within tight tolerances. Levelness, line geometry, surface friction, all measurable, all tested at handover. The school's PE programme depends on the result being playable from week one.

We delivered the court to playing specification under ISO 9001 quality controls. Surface preparation verified before line application. Line markings set with the school's sports coordinator on site. Programme sequenced to give the floor system the cure window it needed.

Court signed off and in service. Trials run on the new surface within the first week. PE programme back to full capacity without a workaround in place.

Constructive thinking.
